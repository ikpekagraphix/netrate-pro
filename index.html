Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate my freelance fees?

Freelance fees are calculated by applying the payment platform's commission, transaction charges, withdrawal fees and applicable currency conversion costs to your earnings. For example, if a platform charges 10%, divide your desired net earnings by 0.90 to calculate the gross amount you should invoice. NetRate & Invoice Pro automates this calculation for popular freelance payment platforms.

How much does Upwork charge freelancers?

Upwork currently charges freelancers a service fee ranging from 0% to 15%, depending on the contract. A 10% fee means you retain 90% of your earnings. If you want to receive $1,000 after a 10% fee, you should charge approximately $1,111.11.

How much should I charge on Fiverr to earn $1,000?

Fiverr freelancers generally receive 80% of their completed order value because the platform retains a 20% commission. To earn $1,000 before withdrawal costs and taxes, you should price your service at $1,250.

How can Nigerian freelancers receive international payments in USD?

Nigerian freelancers can use supported international payment services and virtual USD receiving accounts, including Payoneer, Grey, and Raenest (formerly Geegpay). Each provider has different receiving, withdrawal and currency conversion fees. Always check account eligibility, supported payment routes and applicable charges before choosing a payment method.

How do I invoice international clients without losing money to transaction fees?

Calculate your desired net earnings first, then add the payment processing, withdrawal and currency conversion costs using a reverse-calculation formula. NetRate & Invoice Pro helps freelancers calculate the correct gross invoice amount so they can cover transaction costs and receive their intended payout.

Calculations are estimates for invoicing convenience; verify platform rates and consult a tax professional for official filings.
